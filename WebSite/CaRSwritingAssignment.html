Introduction: Engineering Design and the use of AI Tools

Interest in AI tool use for engineering tasks has been growing rapidly in industry and academia. Recent studies have focused on the complexity of introducing these tools in an academic setting.  The industry changes in this area are rapid and the impacts to engineering education are immediate.  The integration of AI tools into the engineering design process has the potential to boost student creativity, help students push through mind blocks and increase student innovation (Saeidnia & Ausloos, 2024).  Integrating this new technology into engineering design courses will help students prepare for the workforce and increase their ability in the completion of design projects.

Previous studies of AI use in academia have not examined senior engineering students working on Engineering Design projects in groups.  The focus has been on individuals completing design steps on their own. The design process in industry is always a group effort.  In university design courses teams often work together to complete tasks.  Looking at group work to complete engineering design tasks will expand the current research. Assessing how AI tools improve or hinder the group dynamic during design is an interesting area of research. Many current studies on the use of AI tools look at students in an entire university. Such studies are unsatisfactory because they are across all college disciplines without a direct focus on engineering.

For this study we will look at students working on their senior capstone projects. They are working on group projects in engineering design and incorporating engineering tools.  They will complete a questionnaire and then sit down for focus group interviews to discuss their interactions with AI tools.  Women make up one third of the participants in the study. There are 6 participants on each team. There are18 total participants in the study, all of which will answer questions individually and, in a group setting to give insight into group projects in engineering design with the use of AI tools. Two major research questions will guide this study: (1) How does the use of AI technology enhance students' ability to solve complex engineering design tasks and improve their problem-solving effectiveness? and (2) What are the perceived impacts of AI on students' learning, skill development, and ethical considerations in the context of engineering design? There are many potential advantages of incorporation of AI tools in the group design process.  Many students use case-based reasoning or knowledge to aid in the design process.  This is an area where AI could make a big contribution to the process (Guindon & Curtis, 1988).  Self-Regulated Learning (SRL) is a well-documented factor in the design process.  In the SRL process AI tools have the potential to be a great learning partner by helping you adjust their approach when necessary.  These tools can explain what they did to help with the design and move toward the design goal (Afzaal et al., 2024).  Working with AI tools is essential for students so they can critically analyze the output produced by AI and make sure the suggestions are on target.  Exploring the integration of new AI tools into engineering education design courses has many ways it can impact curriculum and industry readiness of engineering graduates.
