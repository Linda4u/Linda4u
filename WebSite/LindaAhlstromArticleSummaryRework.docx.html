Article Summary

The article discusses motivation for ChatGPT adoption in support of active learning (Lai et al., 2023).  The paper thoroughly discusses and evaluates the reasons for adoption and resistance to adoption.  The article includes the students, professors and university perspectives.  The transition stages of adoption in a university setting are well outlined showing initial reactions to the new technology and articulating the progression to complete adoption.  The purpose of the article is to pioneer an endeavor to adapt and extend the Technology Acceptance Model (TAM) to look at direct and indirect impacts on intrinsic motivation in undergraduate students' intentions to use AI technology. The article stated that both intrinsic motivation and perceived usefulness had a strong effect on Behavioral Intention (BI) when using ChatGPT. Lai and others ask two research questions: first, what are factors in undergraduates' intentions to use ChatGPT in active learning and second, how do these factors affect the undergraduates' intentions to use it for answering academic inquiries.

The research method was well defined in the article, it showed the steps taken to collect and analyze the data. Structural Equation Modeling (SEM) was used to identify the association between independent and dependent variables.  A questionnaire was created that was adapted from validated scales outlined in previous studies. These question involved four constructs perceived usefulness, perceived ease of use, intrinsic motivation, and behavioral intensions. Demographic information along with 16 items from the four constructs (four each) were collected from each participant. A five-point Likert scale was used. The authors mentioned that the measurement model was analyzed using Confirmatory Factor Analysis (CFA) to see the relationship between the observed and unobserved variables but did not describe what those variables were. Next the proposed hypothesis was tested for the independent and dependent variables.  The next step in the CFA was to look at the reliability and validity of each construct. Numbers were given for Cronbach's Alpha and all were found to be above the threshold for adequate reliability.  

The findings suggest that overall intrinsic motivation and perceived usefulness have substantial impact on BI. Similar to previous studies, intrinsic motivation is tied to an inner drive to perform a task and the positive satisfaction of learners.  Students are less embarrassed and more comfortable asking the AI tool questions.   The chatbot can encourage students by letting them know it can see they are working hard.  This can protect students’ self-esteem and it also helps students relieve their stress causing students to be motivated to learn.  The article also found that students felt free to keep asking all the questions they wanted without worrying the chatbot was getting exhausted from answering questions.

Implications of the findings were well outlined in the article.  The impact on the extended Technology Acceptance Model (TAM) expansion was discussed.  There was also great discussion of ChatGPT acceptance. The study showed that institutions initially were reluctant and tried to ban the use of the technology citing the problems of plagiarism.  In time one by one they realized what an asset the tools can be in helping learners and reversed their policies in strong adaptation of the new AI technology. The article stresses the importance of future research of the technology to help in its adoption for active learning.  

Lai, C. Y., Cheung, K. Y., & Chan, C. S. (2023). Exploring the role of intrinsic motivation in ChatGPT adoption to support active learning: An extension of the technology acceptance model. Computers and Education: Artificial Intelligence, 5, 100178. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.caeai.2023.100178
