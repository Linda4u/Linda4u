Reflecting on the readings, class discussion, and your personal construction of knowledge from the learning activities, write a response based on the following prompt:

How did analyzing (annotating) your major advisor's research article, contribute to your understanding of the research question(s)?

 

I got a greater appreciation for my advisor's research question by evaluating and critiquing the article.  They asked a great question in their paper: In the process of engineering design, how do experts approach the design problem differently from engineering students?  The investigation into this was quite innovative they designed a one hour session where the participants worked on a project that was not in an area of expertise for them.  They were limited in resources to five websites they could use.  It was interesting to see how students approached the design problem differently than the expert engineers.  The experts had skills for breaking down the problem into subsections.  Their abilities for decomposition and recomposesition of the problem stood out when compared to the students.  The fact that students do not learn these skills during their college years was emphasized.  The data did not show a difference between freshman engineering students and senior engineering students.  The research the team did reconfirmed the findings of previous research.  The difference was that all previous research was done on individual participants.  This article looked at the design process of groups of participants working together.  My understanding of the question on how experts approach the design process differently than students has increased.
