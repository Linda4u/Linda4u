Review your journal reflections throughout the semester and address the following questions:

1. How have your beliefs about academic writing and the writing process evolved?

2. What concepts or strategies from the readings/class lectures have had the most significant impact on your writing?

3. What do you see as current challenges for you as an academic writer? 

4. In what ways do you expect to continue developing your academic writing skills?

 

1. How have your beliefs about academic writing and the writing process evolved?

I am learning fast and feel much more comfortable (I think) than I did when I started last fall. I now understand how things are put together.  There are lots of things that make more sense now, for example secondary sources.  How to work with citing things and avoiding plagiarism.  It is good to look at articles closely and behind the scenes and examine how they are built.  I feel like this exposure will help us in our writing in the future.  We now know how to build articles by closely examining those built by others.  I think it has opened our eyes so we can see that some academic writing is more credible than others.

 

 

2. What concepts or strategies from the readings/class lectures have had the most significant impact on your writing?

I think about the grammar a lot.  I loved that the grammar exercises were tough and seemed to pinpoint all of the places and situations where I am unsure what to do.  I am not sure if I am more confident in grammar or more sure that I don't know what to do in those situations.  I think a deep understanding of how the parts of a paper are put together is helpful.  I think the activities we did that had us look under the covers, so to speak, of an article were helpful.  The lectures were very helpful and memorable.  Grouping and categorizing concepts was a great approach to finding gaps and organizing concepts.  There were many activities that we did that benefited us.  Working in groups was good but I kind of wish we could have moved around through groups.  I enjoyed all of my classmates and would have liked a chance to work with all of them.  

 

3. What do you see as current challenges for you as an academic writer? 

They are large but getting more manageable.  To be honest it was peer pressure that got me to sign up for this class.  I wanted to get the chance to take a class with everyone and that was lots of fun for me.  It was just not like me to voluntarily sign up for a writing class. I knew it would be good for me but I was a bit scared of taking a writing class.  I think this class has boosted my confidence but, my hesitation to take the class was "I am not a good writer so I probably will not do well in the class."  In the end I enjoyed it and the class was fun.  Lots of undergrads told me you will love Professor Schaffer.  I was unsure that it was possible for me to enjoy a writing class but I actually loved it.  

4. In what ways do you expect to continue developing your academic writing skills?

I guess currently I just plan on continuing along the path that writing is a necessary evil and will do it when required.  I think I am just planning to write papers for my research and writing my dissertation but maybe I could see another path other than just trudging through all the things I need to do.  I would need to be enlightened somehow because right now I just see what's in front of me that has to get done as my only path for possible improvement.  I just don't even know a way to continue to work directly on skills I would love to change my ways somehow.  I hated writing my thesis. It was drudgery and I am currently planning to hate writing the dissertation I have looming ahead that I have not even started.
